# /cost-check

Use the `cloud-cost-cutting` skill (provider: `$ARGUMENTS`, or all).

1. Ask for usage exports or read-only access; never billing-admin keys.
2. Inventory resources and monthly cost, flag idle and oversized ones.
3. Propose spend caps and alerts first, then savings with risk, effort and rollback.
4. Make no change and delete nothing without an explicit "yes" per item.
